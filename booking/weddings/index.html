Weddings & Private Events

Live Music for Your Wedding Day

Conor plays live for weddings and private events — vocals and acoustic guitar across Pop, R&B, Country, Hip‑Hop, and the classics, adaptable from a quiet ceremony processional to a reception that gets people dancing. He books within about 100 miles of Pittsburgh, direct, with no marketplace listing fee added to your quote.

Ceremony, Cocktail Hour & Reception

One solo act can cover the whole day: a stripped-back processional and vows, a warm cocktail-hour set while your guests mingle, then a reception set that builds toward the songs everyone actually wants to dance to. No band changeover, no second vendor to coordinate.

Service Area

Home base is Pittsburgh. Bookings typically stay within about 100 miles — the South Hills, North Hills, Washington and Westmoreland counties, plus Morgantown and Wheeling, West Virginia, and Youngstown, Ohio. Venue further out? Ask.

Why Book Direct

Sites like GigSalad, The Bash, and WeddingWire are useful for browsing, but every booking through them carries a platform fee. Book Conor directly here and that fee disappears — and you're emailing the person who'll actually be playing your wedding, not a listing account.

Questions

What does Conor play at a wedding?

Solo vocals and acoustic guitar, spanning Pop, R&B, Country, Hip-Hop, and the classics — built to cover ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception without a band changeover.

How far will he travel?

Most weddings stay within about 100 miles of Pittsburgh — the South Hills, North Hills, Washington and Westmoreland counties, Morgantown and Wheeling WV, and Youngstown OH. Ask about anything further out.

How do we book him?

Use the form on this page or email conorpedersonmusic@gmail.com with your date, venue, and event type. You'll hear back directly from Conor.

Can he learn our first-dance song or take requests?

Send your must-play list when you book — he'll work new material into the set for the songs that matter most to your day.

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have a date. Popular wedding dates fill a venue's calendar well ahead of the event, so reaching out early is the safest move.
