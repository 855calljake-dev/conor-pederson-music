Book Conor Pederson

Conor books weddings, private parties, and college events within about 100 miles of Pittsburgh — the South Hills and North Hills, Washington and Westmoreland counties, out to Morgantown and Wheeling, West Virginia, and Youngstown, Ohio. He performs solo: vocals and acoustic guitar, across Pop, R&B, Country, Hip‑Hop, and the classics.

What Conor Plays

A solo acoustic set — vocals and guitar, no backing band required — built from originals and a wide covers range spanning Pop, R&B, Country, Hip‑Hop, and the classics. The set adapts to the room: quiet and conversational for a ceremony or dinner hour, full-energy for a dance floor or a crowd that wants to sing along.

Service Area

Pittsburgh is home base. Most bookings land within about 100 miles — Pittsburgh proper, the South Hills, North Hills, Washington County, and Westmoreland County, plus Morgantown and Wheeling, West Virginia, and Youngstown, Ohio. A little further out? Ask — travel dates happen too.
