College & Campus Events

Live Music for Your Campus Event

Conor books live sets for fraternity and sorority formals, socials, welcome week, tailgates, and general campus programming — solo vocals and acoustic guitar across Pop, R&B, Country, Hip‑Hop, and the songs your chapter already knows every word to. Available for campuses within about 100 miles of Pittsburgh.

Formals, Socials & Campus Programming

Greek life formals and socials, welcome week, tailgates, homecoming, philanthropy events, campus activities board bookings — a solo acoustic act sets up fast, needs minimal space, and reads the room without a full band's logistics.

Covers Your Crowd Actually Knows, Plus Originals

The set draws from Pop, R&B, Country, Hip‑Hop, and the classics alongside Conor's own released music — built for a crowd that wants to sing along, not sit through a set list they don't recognize.

Campuses in Range

Home base is Pittsburgh, with bookings typically staying within about 100 miles — covering campuses across the Pittsburgh area, Washington County, out to Morgantown, West Virginia, and beyond. That's a service-area radius, not a list of past shows — reach out to check your campus.
