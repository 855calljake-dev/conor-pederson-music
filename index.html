About

Conor Pederson is an artist and songwriter whose dynamic voice shines through in his music, gracefully traversing various genres and sonic pockets.

The Pittsburgh native's warm, textured tone and soulful melodic instinct blend tastefully with sincere, conversational lyricism.

Conor's artistic versatility is on full display at his live shows, where he infuses his unique style and sound into classics spanning various genres and time periods. From Pop to R&B to Country to Hip Hop, the songs you will hear during one of Conor's performances are a portrait of the musical influences that have inspired and ignited his passion for music.
