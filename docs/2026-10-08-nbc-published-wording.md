## NBC's paragraph on Conor, as published

Listed under "Blind Auditions: Night 3". Song line: `"Mercy" by Shawn Mendes`.

> Describing himself as a "finance bro by day" who works in corporate banking, Pederson
> explained that his real passion is music and his Blind Audition was the first time he'd
> performed with drums. Although he didn't get a chair turn, Levine encouraged him to come
> back again and perform an acoustic song next time, while Green suggested he work on
> "timing," something all musicians need to practice.

(The em dashes this file's quotes might have carried are NBC's to use; this quote contains
none. Site copy paraphrases and never adds one.)

## Identity of the coaches

"Levine" is Adam Levine and "Green" is Riley Green: the Season 30 coaching panel is Adam
Levine, Kelly Clarkson, Riley Green and Queen Latifah (verified 2026-10-08 against the
Wikipedia season page's panel listing; Riley Green is a debuting coach this season).

  says it is Conor's call. The page says what he sang and what the coaches told him.

  Voice Season 30" page (checked 2026-10-08 by the briefing session; not re-checked by the
