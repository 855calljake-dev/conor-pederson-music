Running Your Site

You can add shows and releases, swap the two videos on your home page, and edit your About section yourself, any time, from your phone or laptop. No one else has to touch it. Here is everything you need.

What you control

Four parts of the site are yours to edit whenever you want:

  • Upcoming Shows — every date on your calendar.
  • Music — every release, and which one gets the big spotlight at the top.
  • Videos (home page): the two YouTube videos in the Watch section.
  • About section: your bio text and the two photos beside it.

Everything else (the big hero photo, the layout, the booking form) stays fixed unless you ask for a change.

Do this once

Getting your login

  1. Create a GitHub account

    Your site's editor uses GitHub to check it's really you. It's free and takes two minutes. Go to github.com/signup and sign up.

    Use an email you actually check, and write your username and password down somewhere safe. This is the only login you'll ever need for the site.

  2. Accept the invitation

    Once your account exists, send Jake your GitHub username. He'll send an invitation to the email on your GitHub account.

    Open that email and click Accept. Without this, the editor won't let you in.

  3. Log in to the editor

    Go to conorpedersonmusic.com/admin and click Login with GitHub. Approve the permission screen the first time.

    That's it. Bookmark that address — it's where you'll go from now on.

If the address doesn't load

The web address is brand new and can take a day or so to start working everywhere. In the meantime, conorpedersonmusic.netlify.app/admin gets you to exactly the same place.

Adding a show

In the editor, click Upcoming Shows, then Add Shows. Fill in what you know:

Date
The night you're playing.
Time
Written how you'd say it — 6:00 PM. Leave blank if it isn't set yet.
Venue
The room. Gray's on Main.
City
City and state. Franklin, TN.
Ticket label
What shows when there's no ticket link. Usually FREE, but 21+ or Door only work too.
Ticket link
Paste the ticket page address if there is one. Leave it blank and the label above shows instead.

Click Save. If a Publish button appears, click that as well. Your show is on the site in about a minute — refresh the page to see it.

Adding a release

Click Music, then Add Releases.

Title
Exactly how you want it spelled on the site.
Type
Single, EP, or Album.
Release date
The day it drops. This is what sorts your catalog, newest first.
Smart link
Your ffm.to link, or a Spotify or Apple Music link.
Artwork
Upload the cover. Square, and as large as you have it.
Featured
Tick this for the release in the big spotlight at the top.
The one rule to remember

Only one release can be featured at a time. When you tick Featured on a new drop, go back and untick it on the old one. Two featured releases will make the top of your page look wrong.

Changing the videos

Click Videos (home page). There are two slots, Left video and Right video. On a phone, the left one shows first.

YouTube link
Paste the link straight from YouTube. The Share link (youtu.be/…), the normal address (youtube.com/watch?v=…), or a Shorts link all work.
Title
What shows under the video. Leave it blank and the site uses the title from YouTube.
Label
The small tag above the title: Original, Cover, Live. Optional.

Section heading and Intro line are the words above the two videos. Change them or leave them. Click Save and the new video is on the site in about a minute.

Good to know

Videos play right there on your page when someone presses play. Visitors aren't sent off to YouTube, so they stay on your site and keep scrolling to your shows and booking form. On a phone the two sit side by side, and an Expand button under each one takes it full screen.

Editing your About section

Click About section.

Opening line
The big first sentence of your bio.
Paragraphs
The rest of the bio, one paragraph per item. Add Paragraph for a new one, the arrows reorder them, the trash icon removes one.
Tag line
The small boxed label under the bio (VOCALS + ACOUSTIC GUITAR). Leave blank to hide it.
Portrait photo (left)
Upload a tall, upright photo. The site crops it to fit and keeps the upper-middle in view, so a photo where you're centred and not tiny in the frame works best.
Wide photo (bottom right)
Upload a landscape photo.
Describe the photo
A short, plain description of each photo. Screen readers read it aloud and search engines read it too. Conor playing guitar at sunset is plenty.

Changing or removing something

Open Upcoming Shows or Music and you'll see everything already on the site, listed out. Videos and About section open straight to their fields.

  • To change something — click it, edit the field, Save.
  • To remove something — click the trash or minus icon next to it in the list, then Save.
  • To reorder your releases — you don't need to. The release date sorts them for you.

Nothing you do here can break the site's design. The worst case is a typo, and you fix it the same way you made it.

What the site handles on its own

Old shows disappear by themselves. A show stays up through the end of the month it happens in, then drops off. A date on August 25th is visible all the way through August 31st, and is gone on September 1st. You never have to clean up past dates.

Booking requests come to you by email. When someone fills out the form at the bottom of your site, it lands in conorpedersonmusic@gmail.com with their name, email, phone, event date, location, and message. Just reply to it like a normal email.

If something looks wrong

I saved a change and the site still looks the same

Give it two minutes, then hard-refresh the page. On a phone, close the tab and reopen it. If it's still not showing after five minutes, tell Jake.

It won't let me log in

Almost always one of two things: you haven't accepted the emailed invitation yet, or you're signed into a different GitHub account in that browser. Check your email for the invite first, then try again.

The top of my page shows the wrong release

Two releases are ticked Featured, or none are. Open Music, make sure exactly one has Featured ticked, and Save.

My show is missing

Check the date. Shows drop off automatically once their month has ended, so a date entered in the wrong year or month may already have expired.

A video shows a black box or says it's unavailable

Either the link isn't a YouTube video link, or the video is set to Private on YouTube. Open the video on YouTube, use its Share button to copy the link, paste that in, and make sure the video is Public or Unlisted.

My About photo is cropped strangely

Upload a photo where you're near the centre of the frame. Tall/upright photos go in the left slot, wide photos in the right slot. The site crops to fit, so the less empty space around you, the better.

I want to change the big hero photo, the layout, or anything else

Those aren't in the editor on purpose, so they can't get knocked out of place by accident. Send Jake what you want changed and he'll handle it.
