NBC's The Voice · Season 30 · Blind Auditions

First time with drums

Conor took "Mercy" by Shawn Mendes to the Blind Auditions of The Voice. Behind him, for the first time in his life as a performer: drums.

NBC introduced him as a "finance bro by day" who works in corporate banking, and whose real passion is music.

01

The night

The Blind Auditions strip everything away. The coaches sit with their backs to the stage. No face, no story, no stage presence. Just the voice.

Conor put his own spin on "Mercy" and sang it to the backs of four chairs on national television, with drums behind him for the first time. NBC's own recap of the night singles that out: his Blind Audition was the first time he had performed with drums.

02

What the coaches told him

Adam Levine

Encouraged him to come back again, and to perform an acoustic song next time.

Riley Green

Suggested he keep working on "timing," something all musicians need to practice.

As reported in NBC's published recap of the Season 30 Blind Auditions.
